# sources/research/

Your research and fact-verification record. **Read these for facts; never copy
their prose into the manuscript.** This directory is what makes the book's
factual claims auditable and its copyright position defensible.

## What goes here

- **Fact logs** — one file per topic. For each claim you rely on: the fact, the
  primary source (URL, title, edition, page), and the date you checked it. See
  `_TEMPLATE.md`.
- **Permissions and licences** — for any image, font, quotation, or third-party
  material, record what it is, where it came from, and the terms that let you
  use it commercially.
- **Notes distilled from reference material** — facts and structure you took
  from something you read. Facts are not copyrightable; expression is. Take the
  fact, write your own words.

## What does NOT go here

- Cloned third-party books, repos, or docs. Keep those in `reference-clones/`
  (gitignored) or outside the repo entirely. Log what you consulted; do not
  commit the source.
- Anything you would reproduce verbatim in the book.

See `GOVERNANCE.md` for the fact-checking and copyright rules this supports.
