## The scenario

Original media business, already running on Google Cloud, modernising with
generative AI. 250 words.

The distinguishing feature: this customer has already migrated. The problem is
optimisation and AI adoption, not a move. That produces a different question
shape from the classic lift-and-shift scenario, and readers who trained only on
retired case studies will not have seen it.

Stack to include: GKE, media in Cloud Storage, BigQuery warehouse, event-driven
functions, mixed native and third-party monitoring, some remaining on-premises
workflow.

Constraints to state:

- Automated media analysis and metadata generation at scale
- Reliability improvement against a stated objective
- Hybrid Kubernetes, on-premises and cloud, under one management plane
- CI/CD modernisation
