## Exam traps

- "Data must not leave the region" is organization policy plus regional
  resources, not a firewall rule
- "We must hold the keys" is CMEK. "Keys must never sit in Google's systems"
  is External Key Manager
- Exfiltration by an authorised user is VPC Service Controls, not IAM
