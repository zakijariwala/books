## Ingestion

- Pub/Sub for decoupled streaming ingest, delivery guarantees, ordering keys
- Storage Transfer Service and Transfer Appliance for bulk movement
- Datastream for change data capture from operational databases
- The bandwidth arithmetic deciding between wire and appliance

## Processing

- Dataflow as managed Apache Beam, and the batch-plus-stream unification
- Dataproc where an existing Hadoop or Spark estate moves with minimal change
- Cloud Composer for orchestration
- Choosing Dataproc over Dataflow is a migration decision rather than a
  greenfield one

## BigQuery

- Separation of storage and compute, and what that does to cost
- Partitioning and clustering as the two levers that matter
- On-demand against capacity pricing
- Federated queries and external tables
- Materialised views

## Exam traps

- Capturing everything cheaply for unknown future use means Cloud Storage,
  not a warehouse and not a running cluster
- Existing Spark jobs moving as-is points at Dataproc
- Unbounded data with windowing points at Dataflow
