## The strategies

- Rehost, lift and shift
- Replatform, lift and reshape
- Refactor
- Repurchase, retire, retain

Teach the selection criteria rather than the taxonomy. Lease expiry dates and
team capability decide more migrations than technical merit does.

## The programme, not the project

Exam guide section 4 covers business process, and Google's own path skips it.
Cover here:

- Stakeholder management, and who signs off a cutover
- Change management and the communication plan
- Team readiness, with training as a dependency in the plan
- Rollback criteria agreed before the window opens

## Exam traps

- A constraint that the existing stack stays unchanged rules out refactoring,
  however elegant the target
- Datacentre exit dates make rehost correct even where replatform is better
- The right answer to a process question is often a conversation
