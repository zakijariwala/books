## The scenario

Original automotive manufacturer with a connected fleet. 250 words.

Patterns likely to transfer from the retired TerramEarth scenario, to confirm
against the real case study:

- High-volume telemetry ingest from devices in the field
- Intermittent connectivity, and buffering at the edge
- Raw capture of data whose future use nobody has decided yet
- Per-vehicle diagnostic lookup at low latency
- Predictive maintenance built on the collected history

## Working the problem

Two decisions the exam has tested repeatedly in this shape:

- Cheap durable capture of raw data with undecided future value goes to Cloud
  Storage. Streaming everything into a warehouse is the expensive wrong answer
- Low-latency lookup keyed by vehicle and timestamp goes to Bigtable. BigQuery
  serves the analytics, and the split between them recurs

Also cover:

- Pub/Sub ingest, and what ordering guarantees cost
- Dataflow for the processing tier
- Where the model trains, and where it serves
