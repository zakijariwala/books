# Case-study registry

Which example illustrates what, and where. Two jobs: stop any one company being
overused, and make sure every borrowed example is sourced and dated so it can be
re-checked before publication. `/approve` adds rows; `/verify` dates the borrowed
ones.

The **borrowed vs first-hand** column enforces a content rule: borrowed case
studies fill at most half of any chapter's examples. Borrowed examples are what a
reader could find in thirty seconds; the first-hand ones are why they bought the
book.
