# Figures

The running list of figures the book needs, appended by `/approve` from the
`<!-- FIGURE: ... -->` markers in each approved chapter. Nothing is drawn until
Stage 6 (`prompts/stage6-deferred.md`), after three chapters exist — a figure
drawn for a chapter that later gets cut is wasted work.

Figure rules (enforced at draw time): grayscale only, one idea per figure, at
most six labels, readable in isolation, rendered to fit a 6x9 trim.
